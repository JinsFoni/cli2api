### English

- Qoder daily check-in now sends the desktop risk identity (Cosy-MachineToken / Cosy-MachineCode / Cosy-MachineType) generated by the installed Qoder app's runtime-info helper and bound to the account uid. The campaign service only lists the daily credit campaign to requests carrying this triple, so check-in previously reported "no activity" even on days the campaign was live.
- When the daily campaign is not yet listed at the configured check-in time (Qoder rotates it in after 10:00 CST), the scheduler now re-probes every 30 minutes within the same day instead of waiting for tomorrow.

### 中文

- Qoder 每日签到现在会带上本机 Qoder 客户端 runtime-info 组件生成的设备风控身份（`Cosy-MachineToken` / `Cosy-MachineCode` / `Cosy-MachineType`，绑定账号 uid）。活动服务只对携带这套身份的请求列出每日积分活动，此前签到会误报「活动未开放」。
- 配置的签到时间早于活动放出（Qoder 每天北京时间 10:00 后才轮换出签到活动）时，调度器当天每 30 分钟自动重试一次，不再等到第二天。
